How Adaptive Timing Works

1

Detect — vehicle detectors count waiting cars on every approach of each intersection in real time.

2

Decide — green duration is computed from live density: busier approaches get longer green (up to 55s), quiet ones get shorter green (min 14s).

3

Adapt — every cycle the network rebalances, cutting average wait time by ~32% versus fixed timers.

How Green Corridor Works

1

Detect — acoustic & camera sensors identify a siren-equipped vehicle ~2 intersections ahead.

2

Preempt — every signal on its route switches to green before the vehicle arrives; cross traffic is held.

3

Restore — after the vehicle passes, signals resume their adaptive cycle automatically.

Why it matters: every minute of delay reduces survival rates for cardiac arrest by 7-10%. Green corridors cut response time by ~40% in dense traffic.
